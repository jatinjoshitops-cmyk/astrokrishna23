Common Career and Business Concerns

People usually reach out about one of a few things: feeling stuck in the same role for years with no clear next step, an unexpected job loss, tension or disputes with a business partner, a business that's underperforming despite real effort, or simply not knowing whether now is the right time to make a move.

The 10th house governs career and public standing in a Kundli, and it's read alongside the current dasha (planetary period) to understand not just what's happening, but when things are likely to shift. A prolonged Saturn period, for example, often brings a slower, more effortful phase at work that eases once the period changes.

How Krishna Astro Reads Career and Business Charts

Understanding the 10th House and Career Timing

Beyond the 10th house itself, the reading looks at how Saturn, Jupiter and the current dasha are interacting with it — this is usually where the real picture of timing comes from, rather than the 10th house in isolation.

Remedies for Career and Business Growth

Depending on the chart, this could mean a mantra aligned with your career-ruling planet, a favourable window for a job change or a big decision, or a simple remedy to support a partnership that's under strain. Nothing is suggested without first explaining the reasoning behind it.

Should You Start a New Business Now? Timing Matters

Starting a business during a supportive planetary period tends to go more smoothly than starting during a difficult one — timing doesn't replace a sound business plan, but it can meaningfully affect how easily things fall into place early on. This is one of the more frequent questions during a consultation, alongside job-change timing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can astrology tell me the right time to change jobs?

It can point to more and less favourable windows based on your current planetary period, which many people use alongside their own practical assessment of an opportunity.

I'm stuck in the same position for years — can a reading explain why?

Often, yes — a prolonged Saturn or similar period is a common reason behind a stretch of career stagnation, and the chart usually shows when that's likely to ease.

Is this useful for deciding whether to start a business?

Yes, timing is one of the more common reasons people consult before starting something new, alongside their own business planning.

What if my business partner and I are in conflict?

This comes up often. The reading can help clarify what's driving the friction and suggest a more favourable approach or timing for resolving it.

Can you help with financial losses in an existing business?

The consultation can offer perspective on timing and remedial support, though it works best alongside sound financial and business decisions, not instead of them.

Do I need any specific documents or information for a career reading?

Just your date, time and place of birth. No financial documents or business paperwork are needed.

Is this only for business owners, or also for salaried employees?

Both. Career timing and the 10th house apply equally whether you run a business or work a job.

How soon can I expect clarity after the consultation?

Most people leave the session itself with a clearer picture — the timing of any actual change depends on your own decisions and the period involved.

Can astrology help with interview or exam timing?

Yes, this is a common request, and favourable dates can be discussed alongside your preparation.

Is this guidance specific to any particular industry?

No, the approach is based on your individual chart rather than any specific industry, so it applies broadly.
