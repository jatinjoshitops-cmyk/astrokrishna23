Understanding Vedic Astrology and How It Can Help You

What Is Vedic Astrology (Jyotish)?

Vedic astrology, known as Jyotish, is built around your Kundli — a map of where each planet sat in the sky at the exact time and place you were born. Unlike a general sun-sign horoscope, a Kundli reading looks at the houses, planetary positions (grahas) and any doshas — such as Mangal Dosha or Shani-related patterns — that may be shaping a particular area of your life, whether that's marriage, career or health. This is why two people born on the same date can receive very different readings: the exact time and place of birth changes the chart significantly.

How a Kundli-Based Remedy Works

Once a chart has been read against your specific concern, the next step is usually a remedy — this could be a mantra to recite at a particular time, a gemstone suited to a specific planet, a simple pooja, or, in cases involving Vashikaran or suspected negative influence, a more structured remedial process. The goal at Krishna Astro is always to explain why a remedy is being suggested in plain language, so you understand the reasoning rather than simply being told what to do. Remedies are meant to support the situation you're in — they work alongside your own effort and decisions, not instead of them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Vedic astrology and how is it different from Western astrology?

Vedic astrology (Jyotish) uses the sidereal zodiac, which accounts for the slow shift in the Earth's alignment over time, and builds its readings around your exact birth chart rather than just your sun sign. It places heavy emphasis on planetary periods (dashas) and remedies, which Western astrology generally does not use in the same way.

Do I need to visit in person, or can a consultation be done over phone or WhatsApp?

Both are available. Many clients from Surat, Bardoli and nearby towns prefer a phone or WhatsApp consultation for convenience, and it works just as well as an in-person session since the reading is based on your birth details, not physical presence.

What information do I need to provide for a Kundli reading?

Your exact date of birth, time of birth and place of birth. The time of birth matters a lot for accuracy, so it helps to check a birth certificate or hospital record if you're unsure of the exact minute.

Can astrology really help with love and relationship problems?

Astrology can offer clarity on timing, communication patterns and underlying friction shown in your chart, which often helps you approach the relationship with a clearer head. It works best alongside your own effort and honest conversation, not as a replacement for either.

Is my consultation kept confidential?

Yes. Personal and family matters discussed during a consultation, whether by phone, WhatsApp or in person, are treated as private.

How does Kundli matching work before marriage?

Kundli Milan compares both partners' charts across a set of compatibility points, commonly including the Guna Milan system, along with a check for doshas such as Mangal Dosha. The result is explained point by point so both families understand what it means in practice, not just a final score.

What kind of remedies do you typically suggest?

Depending on the chart and the concern, remedies may include a specific mantra, a recommended gemstone, a simple pooja, or fasting on a particular day. Every remedy is explained clearly so you understand why it's being suggested.

Do you serve clients outside Bardoli and Surat?

Yes. While Krishna Astro is based to serve Bardoli and Surat closely, phone and WhatsApp consultations mean clients from nearby towns across South Gujarat can be guided just as easily.
