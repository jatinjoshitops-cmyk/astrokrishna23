Marriage Problem Solution — Guidance for a Stronger Marriage

Marital friction, in-law conflict, communication breakdown, or a marriage that keeps getting delayed — each of these shows up in a Kundli, and each has a practical, explained remedy behind it.

Common Marriage Problems We See

Marriage-related consultations tend to fall into a few categories: ongoing friction between spouses, conflict with in-laws that spills into the marriage itself, communication that has broken down over time, disagreements about money or major decisions, and marriage that keeps getting delayed for reasons that aren't always clear.

In Vedic astrology, marital harmony is read primarily through the 7th house and its ruling planet, along with any doshas — Mangal Dosha being the most commonly discussed — and the current planetary period (dasha) a person is passing through. A difficult Saturn or Rahu period, for instance, often coincides with a stretch of friction that eases once the period changes.

Photo: a family gathering with in-laws present

How Krishna Astro Approaches Marriage Problems

Reading the 7th House and Beyond

Beyond the 7th house itself, the reading looks at how it's being influenced by other planets and houses — this is often where the real explanation for a specific pattern of conflict shows up, rather than in the 7th house alone.

Remedies for Marital Harmony

Depending on what's found, this might mean a specific mantra for one or both spouses, a small ritual timed to a particular day, or guidance on when to have a difficult conversation rather than avoid it. Where in-laws are the main source of tension, the approach usually focuses on practical timing and communication rather than confrontation.

Delayed Marriage: What the Chart Can Show

For unmarried adults facing repeated delays — proposals falling through, families unable to agree, or simply no progress despite looking — the 7th house and the current dasha usually point to a reason, and often to a window when things tend to move more easily. This is one of the more common reasons people first get in touch with Krishna Astro.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can astrology explain why my marriage is delayed?

Often, yes. The 7th house and your current planetary period together usually point to a reason, and to a general window when progress tends to come more easily.

My spouse and I keep arguing over small things — can a reading help?

It can help identify whether there's a specific planetary influence behind the pattern, and suggest timing or remedies that support a calmer approach at home.

Is Mangal Dosha the reason for problems in my marriage?

Not necessarily — it's one factor among several that get checked. It's explained clearly whether it's relevant to your specific chart rather than assumed by default.

Can this help if my in-laws are the main source of conflict?

Yes, this is a common reason for consultation. The guidance usually focuses on practical timing and communication approaches rather than confrontation.

Should both spouses attend the consultation, or is one enough?

One spouse is enough to begin with. Having both is useful for a fuller picture but isn't required.

What if we're already considering separation?

That's discussed honestly. Sometimes the chart points toward working through a difficult period, and sometimes it supports the clarity you're already feeling — both are addressed directly.

Are the remedies you suggest specific to Vedic tradition, or can anyone follow them?

They're rooted in Vedic tradition, but they're simple enough — a mantra, a specific timing, a small ritual — that anyone can follow them regardless of background.

How is this different from a Kundli matching reading before marriage?

This service is for an existing marriage that's facing difficulty. Kundli matching is a compatibility check typically done before marriage — see the Kundli Reading & Matching page.

Can you help if the marriage was arranged and we barely know each other?

Yes — this comes up often, and the reading can help both of you understand each other's patterns and timing better as the marriage settles.

Is my situation kept private if my spouse doesn't want to be involved?

Yes, whatever you share stays confidential whether or not your spouse chooses to take part.
