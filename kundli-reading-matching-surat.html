Kundli Reading & Matching for Surat Families

Kundli matching before marriage is a common request from Surat families, and scheduling often has to work around both families' busy lives — sessions are frequently split across an initial phone consultation followed by an in-person meeting once a date is confirmed.

The reading covers the standard Ashtakoot (Guna Milan) system alongside a check for Mangal Dosha and other key doshas, explained clearly rather than reduced to a single number.

What a Surat Matching Session Typically Involves

Working Around Two Busy Families

Phone consultations are often used for an initial read of both charts, with an in-person session available afterward if both families want to go through the result together.

When the Score Isn't Straightforward

A lower Guna Milan score doesn't automatically rule out a marriage — several doshas can be offset with specific remedies, explained honestly rather than treated as a dealbreaker.

Photo: two charts compared side by side

Booking a Session in Surat

Bring your date, time and place of birth — and your partner's for a matching session. In-person, phone and WhatsApp consultations are all available, including evening slots.

Photo: a family consultation session

Reading a Kundli Report Together

Many Surat families arrive at a matching session already holding a printed report from an online tool, unsure what to make of a specific dosha listed on it. Going through that report line by line — rather than starting over from scratch — is a common and perfectly reasonable way to use a session, especially when the concern is really about one or two specific points rather than the whole reading.

This is particularly common with Mangal Dosha, which shows up on a lot of standard reports but varies enormously in how much it actually matters once the rest of both charts are considered together. A phone consultation beforehand is often enough to work through this before deciding whether an in-person follow-up is even needed. Sending a clear photo of the report over WhatsApp ahead of the call also helps, so the specific points in question can be looked at directly rather than described secondhand.
