What a Full Kundli Reading Covers

A Kundli reading maps where each planet was positioned at your exact time and place of birth, then reads that against the twelve houses that govern different areas of life — career, marriage, health, finance, and more. It also looks at your current and upcoming planetary periods (dashas), which is usually where the most useful, time-specific guidance comes from.

Because the reading is built from your exact birth details, two people born on the same day can receive quite different readings once time and place of birth are factored in.

Photo: a close-up of a Kundli chart

Kundli Matching (Milan) Before Marriage

The Guna Milan System, Explained

Kundli Milan typically uses the Ashtakoot system, comparing both charts across eight categories for a score out of 36. Alongside this, a check for Mangal Dosha and a few other key doshas is included, since these can matter more than the overall score on their own.

What Happens If the Match Isn't Perfect

A lower Guna Milan score doesn't automatically mean a marriage won't work — certain doshas can be offset with specific remedies, and the score is only one part of a fuller reading. This is explained honestly, point by point, rather than reduced to a single pass-or-fail number.

Photo: two birth charts placed side by side for matching

What to Bring to a Kundli Reading or Matching Session

For an individual reading, your date, time and place of birth are enough. For matching, the same details for both partners are needed. Sessions work equally well in person, by phone, or over WhatsApp, so families in Bardoli, Surat or further afield can take part without needing to travel.

Frequently Asked Questions

What information do you need for a Kundli reading?

Your exact date, time and place of birth. The time of birth matters a lot for accuracy, so check a birth certificate or hospital record if you're unsure of the exact minute.

What is Guna Milan and how many points matter?

Guna Milan is the Ashtakoot compatibility system, scored out of 36 across eight categories. Higher scores are generally favourable, but specific doshas are checked alongside the score, not instead of it.

What if our Kundli matching score is low — does that mean we shouldn't marry?

Not necessarily. A lower score is explained point by point, and certain doshas can be offset with specific remedies — it's rarely a simple pass-or-fail situation.

Can a reading be done without an exact birth time?

It's possible, but accuracy improves significantly with an exact time. If you're unsure, checking a birth certificate or hospital record beforehand is worth the effort.

How long does a full Kundli reading take?

A typical session runs long enough to cover your main areas of interest in detail — this is discussed when you book.

Is Kundli matching only for arranged marriages?

No, it's just as commonly requested by couples who've chosen each other and want a compatibility check before finalising marriage plans.

What is Mangal Dosha and does it always cause problems?

Mangal Dosha refers to a specific placement of Mars in the chart. It doesn't automatically cause problems — its effect depends on the rest of the chart, and remedies exist where it is a genuine concern.

Can I get a Kundli reading for my child?

Yes, this is a common request, usually focused on general life direction, education, or health tendencies rather than marriage-specific topics.

Do both families need to be present for a matching session?

No, it's usually just the two individuals or their immediate decision-makers — whatever is comfortable for your family.

How is this different from an online automated Kundli matching tool?

An automated tool gives a raw score with no context. Here, every point is explained, including what a specific dosha or low score actually means for your situation.
