What Happens After You Reach Out

However you get in touch — phone, WhatsApp, or in person — the first step is always the same: explaining your situation in your own words. Pricing and scheduling are confirmed directly during that first conversation, since they depend on which service fits and whether it's a single reading or something that benefits from a follow-up. If it turns out a different service fits better once you've explained the situation, that's sorted out then — not something you need to figure out in advance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which service is right for me?

Explain your situation over a call or WhatsApp, and the right service will be discussed with you directly.

Can one consultation cover more than one concern?

Yes, if several things are connected — for example, a relationship problem tied to family opposition — they're usually discussed together in one session.

Are the Bardoli and Surat pages different services, or the same ones?

Same underlying services, with content and scheduling reflecting local availability for in-person consultations.

Is Kundli Reading & Matching only for people about to marry?

No, it also covers general birth chart readings for career, health and other life areas.

Do all services cost the same?

Please get in touch directly to discuss consultation details for your specific situation.

Can I switch services mid-consultation if it turns out something else fits better?

Yes, this happens often and is handled without any issue.

Are these services available outside Bardoli and Surat?

Yes, phone and WhatsApp consultations are available for clients across South Gujarat and beyond.

Is there a service specifically for family disputes unrelated to marriage?

Family-related concerns are usually covered under Vashikaran Expert or Black Magic Expert, depending on the specifics — this is discussed during the initial conversation.

Do you offer a combined package covering more than one service?

Consultations are tailored to your situation rather than sold as fixed packages — get in touch to discuss what fits.

What if my situation doesn't seem to fit any of these categories?

Reach out anyway — most situations fit somewhere once they're discussed, and if not, that's addressed honestly too.
